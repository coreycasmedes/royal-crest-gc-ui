## The work

- A full roof replacement with a 30-year CertainTeed shingle
- Half-round copper gutters with copper gutter screens
- Wood trim repairs, including a complete fascia replacement
- Wood trim repairs to the front columns
- A copper metal canopy above the front door
- Custom fabricated louvered window shutters on the front of the home

## Built for a historic home

Every part of the job was done with the home's historic character in mind.
