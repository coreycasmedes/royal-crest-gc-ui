Our Inspection and Installation Process

  1. 01

    Inspection

    We inspect the roof and recommend a repair or a replacement if one is needed.

  2. 02

    Insurance or Estimate

    For a replacement, you weigh the pros and cons of going through insurance. Otherwise you approve the estimate, scope of work and materials.

  3. 03

    Permit

    Once insurance approves the roof and funds arrive, or once you approve the estimate, we pull the permit.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Most roofs take one day, and larger homes up to three. Gutters come off the day before and go back on the day after.

  5. 05

    Cleanup

    We sweep the front and back yards with nail magnets at least four times and check with the neighbors on both sides.

  6. 06

    Walkthrough and Payment

    We walk the property with you, and collect payment only once the work is done and you are happy with it.

Roofing Questions

What exactly is replaced during a roof replacement?

Everything from the roofline up, except the roof deck itself and any stone or masonry rework. Up to three sheets of decking are included at no extra cost to repair rotted deck we find while working.

How long will the project take?

Typically one to three days, depending on the size of the home. About 90% of the time we finish in one day, with the gutters coming off the day before and going back on the day after.

When is payment collected?

After all the work is complete and we have walked through it with you to make sure everything meets our standards and you are happy.

How do you protect my home during the work?

We bring 8 to 15 sheets of decking and sawhorses to protect your windows and landscaping. A Spanish-speaking team member is on site to coordinate with the crew and help protect the property.

How clean will my property be afterward?

We sweep the front and back yards with nail magnets at least four times, and we check with the neighbors on both sides.

Should I go through insurance?

After the inspection we walk you through the pros and cons of an insurance claim for your situation, so you can decide.

Which areas do you serve?

Highland Park, University Park, Lakewood, Lake Highlands, Dallas, Richardson, Frisco, Plano. We always come to you.
